## Purpose
SIG: Game Intelligence exists to cultivate a community of practice around intelligent techniques for games and interactive systems.

## Scope
We welcome:
- Game AI (planning, behavior trees, utility AI, search, RL)
- Player modeling and game analytics
- Procedural content generation (PCG/PCGML)
- Simulation and evaluation methodology
- Tooling and pipelines for intelligent game systems

## Operating model
- Weekly session (talks / demos / reading group)
- Quarterly workshop or mini-event
- Yearly competition and maybe publication

## Weekly sessions
- **Reading day**: 1 paper + 1 applied take away
- **Demo day**: showcase of agent bot + improvement discussion
- **Build day**: implement a baseline agent / analytics pipeline together

## Example themes
- RL baselines for small games (gridworlds, Atari-style, Unity ML-Agents)
- Behavior trees vs utility AI in modern games
- PCG for levels, quests, and itemization
- Telemetry design and player segmentation

## How to join a session
Just show up to one of the sessions. Please check **[Events]({{ '/events/' | relative_url }})** for upcoming sessions and activities.
