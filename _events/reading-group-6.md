## Topic
**Review and Recap**

We will discuss on how to improve policy-based reinforcement learning on Tic-Tac-Toe game. Then, we will look into Pokemon VGC and its recent progress toward superhuman level.

---

## Agenda

- **15 minutes — Review and Recap**
  - Quick recap of policy-based reinforcement learning
  - Review of the Tic-Tac-Toe implementation (policy network, training loop, results)
  - Key lessons learned from the experiment

- **20 minutes — Improving the Tic-Tac-Toe Agent**
  - Discussion of possible improvements
  - Exploration strategies and entropy regularization
  - Better baselines and training stability
  - Ideas for extending the experiment

- **15 minutes — From Tic-Tac-Toe to Real Games**
  - Introduction to Pokémon VGC as a reinforcement learning environment
  - Key challenges: large action space, partial observability, stochastic outcomes
  - Overview of recent research and progress toward superhuman play

- **10 minutes — Open Discussion**
  - Research directions for Game AI within the group
  - Ideas for future experiments and competitions

## Notes
This session is informal and discussion-oriented. Participants are encouraged to ask questions, share ideas, and actively experiment during the workshop.
